03 / RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS

What are we testing?

WORKING HYPOTHESIS
Intents is generating revenue, but capture efficiency and token supply mechanics matter more than gross volume alone.

Preliminary finding: October reported higher net Intents revenue despite lower fee volume and TVL than September. This supports an improving capture thesis, not necessarily a rising token price. An independently queried NEAR network transaction series shows daily average transactions fell 8.8% from September to October; unique signers and contract breadth remain unmeasured.

+
Evidence that strengthens the thesis

Broad signer growth, wider contract activity and defensible evidence of economic value capture.

−
Evidence that weakens the thesis

A declining fee base, validator emissions, operational risk, and weak retention could outweigh improved fee capture.

?
What we cannot assume

Unique wallets are not unique people. Gross volume is not revenue. Correlation is not causation.
